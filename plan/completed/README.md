# Completed Platform plans

This directory contains immutable PF Slice plans that have passed their
required implementation, independent review, independent acceptance and
exact-candidate validation gates.

Do not edit a terminal plan to absorb later work. If a delivered Slice needs a
new repair or materially changed contract, create or supersede an active plan,
preserve the historical plan, and record the relationship in the tracker and
implementation reconciliation.

PF-B1 foundation Slices, the complete PF-B2 identity/authentication Batch, the
complete PF-B3 Organisation authority Batch, the complete PF-B4 Workspace
authority Batch, the complete PF-B5 product-access Batch, the complete PF-B6
product assertion/consumer Batch, PF-B7-S01/S02 and PF-B8-S01/S02 are
terminally implemented here. PF-B7 and PF-B8 are certified; PF-B9-S01 is the
next active Slice.
Planning, repository setup and validation scaffolding do not by themselves
make a Slice Implemented.
